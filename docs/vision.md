# TacticalMind AI Vision

## Overview

TacticalMind AI is an interactive football intelligence platform. It is not a game. It is a tactical simulator that models football as a system of interacting intelligent agents. Users can analyze matches, compare tactical philosophies, simulate formations, and understand why tactical decisions emerge.

## Core Principles

- **Interactive tactical board** - Manipulate formations and see outcomes
- **Multi-agent AI** - Players make intelligent decisions based on roles and positions
- **Explainable tactical decisions** - Understand the "why" behind AI choices
- **Team identities** - Model distinct tactical philosophies (e.g., Scaloni's Relationism, Spain's Positional Play)
- **Passing networks** - Visualize and analyze passing patterns
- **Space control** - Map dominant areas and pressure zones
- **Pressing simulations** - Test defensive strategies
- **Tactical replay** - Review and analyze simulated scenarios
- **"What if?" analysis** - Explore hypothetical tactical changes

## Target Audience

- Football analysts and coaches
- Tactical enthusiasts
- Students of the game
- Anyone interested in understanding football as a complex system
